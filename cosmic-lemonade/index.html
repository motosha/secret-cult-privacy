Cosmic Lemonade — Privacy information
Effective date: 21 September 2026

Local gameplay
Cosmic Lemonade is an offline single-player game. It does not require an account and has no advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases or developer-operated cloud save service. The game does not send your station, save files or gameplay activity to the developer.

What is stored
Station names you choose, game progress, save timestamps, tutorial progress and audio preferences are stored in the app's local storage. This information is used to resume your game and retain your preferences. Music and sound effects are included with the game; playback may create local temporary cache files.

Retention and removal
Saves remain until overwritten or removed with the app's local data. On Android, you can remove local data in the system app settings or by uninstalling the app. Your operating system may independently back up or restore app data according to your device and backup settings. Those backups are managed by your operating-system provider, not by a Cosmic Lemonade cloud service. In a web build, clearing the site's storage removes local saves.

Optional support
Copy diagnostics only copies a short report to your clipboard when you press the button. It includes the app version, platform, save format and, when a station is open, its tier, module count and active tick count. It does not include your station name or save contents. Nothing is sent automatically. If you choose to contact support, the recipient will receive the information and attachments you send and use them to answer your request. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Third-party software and store services
The app uses Flutter, local Hive storage and bundled audio playback components. Their notices are available under Open-source licenses. Google Play purchases, device backups and optional operating-system diagnostics are governed by the relevant provider's terms and settings; this app does not add a separate tracking service.

Contact
The publisher and support contact provided for the installed release are displayed in Help & about and in its store listing.

This privacy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. This concerns visits to this website; the offline game does not connect to this website automatically. This page includes no analytics, advertising, forms, external fonts or embedded media.
